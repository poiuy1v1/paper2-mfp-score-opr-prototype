# Scoring admission gate

## Decision

**HOLD / NOT ADMITTED**

No source-grounded MFP scores or OPR rankings are generated. All records retain `scoring_admitted = false`.

## Reasons

- Required descriptors remain incomplete.
- Surface tension, contact angle and pore-size semantics are not compatible across synthesis-state and post-synthesis measurements.
- Framework stability score is undefined.
- No approved derived-value or external-lookup policy is active.
- Source concentration has improved but the registry is not an independent benchmark or training set.
