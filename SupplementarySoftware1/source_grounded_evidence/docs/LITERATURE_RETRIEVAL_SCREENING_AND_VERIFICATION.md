# Literature retrieval, screening and verification

This is a targeted source-grounded evidence assembly, not an exhaustive systematic review. The final search/update date was 2026-07-29. Publisher pages, DOI landing pages, institutional repositories, arXiv, Google Scholar and bibliographic metadata services were used. Primary records required an identifiable material, explicit processing condition, explicit sample-form outcome and stable source locator. Reviews were discovery aids only. One paper could contribute multiple records only when the material-condition-outcome unit differed. Powder/failure outcomes remain condition-specific. AI assisted retrieval/extraction/consistency checks; Jinnan Wei manually verified 49/49 records. Any correction requires a versioned changelog.
